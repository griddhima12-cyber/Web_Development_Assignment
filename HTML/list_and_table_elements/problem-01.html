GLA University Admission Handbook

Admission Overview

GLA University Greater Noida offers various undergraduate and postgraduate programmes. This admission handbook provides information about eligibility, application process, required documents, important terms, fee structure and contact details.

Eligibility

Students must satisfy the eligibility requirements of the programme they wish to apply for.

  • Candidate must have the required educational qualification.
  • Candidate must satisfy the minimum marks requirement.
  • All mandatory documents must be submitted.
  • Candidate must complete the admission process on time.

Application Deadline: Check the official GLA admission portal for the latest deadline.

Application Process

Follow these steps to complete the admission process:

  1. Register online on the GLA University admission portal.
  2. Pay the registration fee.
  3. Appear for the GLA Entrance Test (GLAET), if applicable.
  4. Check the GLAET result.
  5. Appear for the interview, if required.
  6. Pay the required token amount.
  7. Upload the required documents.

Required Documents

  1. Class 10 marksheet
  2. Class 12 marksheet
  3. Transfer Certificate
  4. Migration Certificate, if applicable
  5. Government-issued identity proof
  6. Recent passport-size photographs
  7. Required admission and fee documents

Important Terms

Eligibility
Conditions that a student must satisfy to apply for a programme.
Merit List
A list of students prepared according to the applicable admission criteria.
GLAET
GLA Entrance Test conducted for applicable admission processes.
Counselling
A process that helps students with programme selection and admission.
Scholarship
Financial assistance provided to eligible students.
Registration
The process of submitting details to begin an admission application.
